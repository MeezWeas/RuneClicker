Boss ladder

Your seven combat skills fight for you around the clock. A boss must go down within 60 seconds, so if your damage is too low the fight never finishes. Every kill pays gp and has a 1 in 8 chance at the boss's unique drop, which adds 3% to all XP and gp for good.

Quests

Each quest is done once and its reward is permanent, even through prestige.

Achievements

Each achievement adds 1% to all XP and gp, permanently.

Pets

Every action has a tiny chance to find that skill's pet, and the chance grows with your level. A pet adds 10% XP and gp in its skill, permanently.

Prestige

Start over on a fresh account and carry Renown with you. Each point of Renown adds 5% to all XP and gp, forever. You need total level 1,000 to prestige, and the more XP you earned this run, the more Renown you get.

You keep: Renown, achievements, pets, quest rewards, boss drops and stats. You lose: gp, levels, tools, automation, stockpiles, and the clicking and event upgrades.

Save data and reset

The game saves in this browser every 10 seconds. Automation keeps earning for up to 8 hours while the page is closed. To move your save, export it, copy the text, and import it elsewhere.
